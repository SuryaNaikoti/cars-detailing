## 2. Strongest Demonstration Workflows

1. **The Modern Customer Intake Journey (Front-of-House to Back-of-House):**
   - Start on the Public Website.
   - Use the **What Do You Drive?** selector to qualify a 2023 Porsche 911 Carrera.
   - Submit a diagnostic enquiry.
   - Switch to the Workshop Control Center: Show the lead arriving instantly in **Leads & Enquiries**.
   - Qualify the lead, convert it to a scheduled appointment, and execute the arrival check-in with a single click to generate **Job Card JC-2057**.
   - *Impact on Workshop Owner:* Demonstrates frictionless lead capture and elimination of manual double-entry.

2. **The Digital Quote & Customer Decision Portal:**
   - Open `/quote/demo-quote-porsche`.
   - Walk through the interactive line-item breakdown (Mandatory vs Recommended items).
   - Show how declining a recommended brake fluid flush automatically adjusts the customer's total in real time.
   - Submit the decision, then navigate back into **Reminders & Follow-Ups** in the Workshop OS to reveal the newly generated **CF-01 Declined Recommendation Follow-Up** card.
   - *Impact on Workshop Owner:* Proves that deferred repair recommendations are never forgotten, directly maximizing customer lifetime value.

3. **Workshop Floor & Live Technician Balancing:**
   - Display the high-density **Workshop Floor** bay layout (`BAY 01` to `BAY 04`).
   - Move a Job Card to another bay or reassign a technician.
   - Instantly show the **Technicians & Workforce** view update the specialist's assigned job count and overload indicators.
   - *Impact on Workshop Owner:* Visualizes bay capacity and prevents technician overloading.

## 4. Cautionary Guidelines for Demonstrators

1. **Do not claim automated WhatsApp API delivery:** Explicitly describe the WhatsApp button as a "one-click contextual advisor quick link" rather than an autonomous cloud bot.
2. **Do not open side-by-side tabs expecting real-time live synchronization:** Explain that full multi-tablet live socket synchronization is part of the cloud backend rollout.
3. **Praise the Data Honesty:** Point out that the Reports and Analytics modules strictly calculate "Approved Scope" and "Parts vs Labour" rather than claiming artificial profit or unearned margins. Workshop owners deeply respect this financial honesty.
